Stop writing long null checks

Optional chaining + nullish coalescing replace a whole chain of && checks. Bonus: ?? won't throw away valid falsy values like 0 or an empty string.

Optional chaining stops at null/undefined. ?? only falls back on null/undefined, so 0 and "" survive.
